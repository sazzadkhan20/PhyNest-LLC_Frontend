At PHYNEST LLC, we value transparency, trust, and your privacy. As a property maintenance and preservation provider committed to clear communication and responsible data handling, this Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage your information when you interact with us through our website, email, phone, or SMS.

By using our services or visiting this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to deliver efficient, quality service. This may include:

  • Full name
  • General location (for example, city or ZIP code)
  • Phone number and email address
  • Service request details you voluntarily submit

We do not collect Social Security numbers, payment card details, or other highly sensitive data through our public-facing website.

2. Why We Collect It

We use your information to:

  • Respond to service requests and general inquiries
  • Coordinate appointments, work orders, and field visits
  • Provide SMS or text notifications when you opt in
  • Improve communication and day-to-day operations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information for third-party marketing purposes.

3. SMS Communication Policy

You may choose to receive service-related updates by SMS. If you consent, messages may include:

  • Job status updates
  • Appointment confirmations or reminders
  • Notifications about property access or delays

Message details

  • Message frequency: up to 2 messages per week
  • Carrier charges: standard message and data rates may apply
  • Sender number: SMS may come from our business line at (301) 547-3618

Manage SMS preferences

  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message
  • Help: reply HELP or contact us at phynestllc@gmail.com

Phone numbers collected for SMS use are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing.

4. Use of Cookies and Site Analytics

We may use basic cookies or analytics tools to understand how visitors use our website and to improve performance. These tools help us refine your experience and are not used to collect sensitive personal identifiers.

You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings.

5. Information Security

We follow industry-standard practices to help safeguard your data, including:

  • Encrypted website connections (SSL/TLS) when hosted securely
  • Internal access controls
  • Secure storage practices for business communications
  • Periodic review of our digital workflows

No system can guarantee complete security. By using our services, you acknowledge that residual risk remains.

6. Your Privacy Rights

As a client or site visitor, you may:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to update or correct inaccurate details
  • Request deletion of information we no longer need to retain

To exercise these rights, email phynestllc@gmail.com.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect or store personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If such information is submitted in error, we will delete it promptly after we are notified.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect service changes or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
